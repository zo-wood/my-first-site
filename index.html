A book I love

The Hunger Games

by Suzanne Collins

Summary

In the nation of Panem, built on the ruins of North America, the powerful Capitol rules over twelve poor districts. Every year, each district must send one boy and one girl to fight to the death in the Hunger Games, a televised event meant to remind everyone who is in charge.

When her little sister Prim is chosen, sixteen-year-old Katniss Everdeen of District 12 volunteers to take her place. Thrown into the arena with Peeta Mellark, the baker's son, Katniss has to rely on her hunting skills and her wits to stay alive, all while figuring out who she can trust and how to play to the cameras.

Three reasons I recommend it

  • 1

    It's impossible to put down

    Short chapters and cliffhanger endings make every page pull you into the next one.

  • 2

    Katniss is an unforgettable heroine

    She's tough, resourceful, and fiercely loyal to her family, but she's also unsure of herself, which makes her feel real.

  • 3

    It makes you think

    Beneath the action, it raises real questions about power, inequality, and how entertainment can make people stop noticing cruelty.
